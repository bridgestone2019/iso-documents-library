# ISO Documents Library

A public webpage that lists ISO documents stored in your Google Drive folder.

## Drive folder

Parent folder: [ISO Documents Library](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1UpRm39r_GkT3QOT_qlUyOOROWPnBK370)

Subfolders:

- ISO 9000 Quality Management
- ISO 14000 Environmental
- ISO 27000 Information Security
- ISO 45000 Occupational Health and Safety
- ISO 17025 Laboratory Competence
- Other ISO Standards

## Make the documents viewable

1. Open the parent folder in Google Drive.
2. Click Share.
3. Under General access, choose Anyone with the link.
4. Role: Viewer.
5. Click Done.

Until this is done, visitors see a Google sign-in page instead of files.

## Upload documents

Drag PDFs into the matching subfolder. They appear on the webpage automatically.

Share only copies you have the right to distribute. Official ISO standards are copyrighted.
